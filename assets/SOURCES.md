# Game artwork sources

Downloaded October 16, 2026 from public publisher product pages and rulebooks. Artwork belongs to the respective publishers and artists. Attribution is not a claim of ownership, endorsement, or an open license.

Images are kept locally for offline use. `game-media.js` displays selected regions of unchanged rulebook-page images through SVG viewBoxes. Simplified layouts and explanations remain Little Table's own. Photos and boxes may show a different printing or language from a player's copy.

## Skull King

Grandpa Beck’s Games; illustration by Apryl Stott; design by Brent Beck, Tauni Beck, and Jeff Beck.

- [Official rules and FAQ](https://www.grandpabecksgames.com/pages/skull-king)
- `skull-cover.jpg`: [publisher box image](https://www.grandpabecksgames.com/cdn/shop/files/1_7a70f8b3-1205-40d4-9674-484160045c55.jpg?width=800)
- `skull-components.jpg`: [publisher card photo](https://www.grandpabecksgames.com/cdn/shop/files/3.jpg?width=1200)
- `skull-hierarchy.jpg`: [official hierarchy reference](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0565/3230/4053/files/SK_Heirarchy.jpg?v=1672938216). Used for numbered-suit symbols.
- `skull-reference-10.png`, `skull-reference-11.png`: pages 10 and 11 from the [publisher-linked current English rulebook](https://cdnm.heyzine.com/files/uploaded/46b5bd7166a9f579404e175e4990c9d415b4aac7.pdf), copyright 2012, 2021, 2024. Used for Pirate, Tigress, Skull King, Mermaid, and Escape card art.

Reference PNGs were rendered with Mozilla PDF.js 3.11.174 as a temporary preparation tool. The renderer is not shipped or loaded by the app; no runtime dependency was added. Only the pictured regions are shown, not full rulebook pages.
